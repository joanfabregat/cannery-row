# Weekday moving average (retrospective)

*Written after the fact from the notebook and the run's metrics file.*

The forecast for a given shop, product and day is the mean of the same weekday's sales over the previous four weeks. It was backtested on 13 weekly windows (480 shop-product series).

| Horizon | Seasonal naive | Four-week average |
| --- | --- | --- |
| overall | 1.00 | 0.93 |
| 1 day | 1.00 | 0.90 |
| 7 days | 1.00 | 0.96 |

The gain comes from smoothing out one-off days (a closed shop, a local event). It loses on the week after a public holiday, when the average still includes the holiday's low sales.
